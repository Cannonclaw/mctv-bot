# Grove Collective — Voice & Identity Brief

Reconstructed from a four-angle search sweep of their public footprint
(X @grovecollectnil, Instagram @grovecollectivenil, press embeds, campaign
coverage). Direct access to their site and socials was network-blocked, so
everything here came through search snippets and press quotes — verbatim
where marked, paraphrase where not. Confirm with the client before any of
it goes to air.

## How they actually talk

Recovered verbatim post patterns:

- **"Good morning, Rebel Nation‼️"** — their standard hype opener.
  "Rebel Nation" is always direct address, never third person.
- **"[Player] is in the SIP!"** — signing-announcement formula. "The Sip"
  = Mississippi; "in the SIP" = arrived in Oxford.
- **"Grove Collective Members ‼️"** — member-benefit callout opener.
- **"REBS‼️"** — all-caps urgency opener for drops and auctions.
- **"Hotty Toddy!"** — the sign-off. They end announcements with it
  (e.g. the Juice Kiffin post). Spot 04's closing CTA uses it.
- **"$1 for every point when your Rebs WIN"** — pledge-drive phrasing;
  note "your Rebs."
- The ‼️ double exclamation is their signature punctuation. They use
  🔴🔵 as a color sign-off pair. **They do not use hashtags.**

Positioning lines (site/bio, verbatim):

- "an Ole Miss exclusive NIL program created to support and elevate Ole
  Miss athletes" — the account bio. (Our lockup kicker "OLE MISS
  EXCLUSIVE NIL" matches this language.)
- "the team behind the team" — membership tagline.
- "NIL done right — right by the law and right by Ole Miss student
  athletes" — About-page core positioning.

## Visual identity

- **Colors (confirmed):** "Rebel Red" and "Ole Miss Powder Blue" — named
  explicitly in their apparel-line press. Navy rides along via Ole Miss
  athletics. Our campaign palette matches; still swap exact hexes when
  the client provides them.
- **Logo: UNCONFIRMED.** It exists (appears on shirts and caps) but no
  recoverable source describes the mark. Do not fabricate it — keep the
  type lockup until they send the file.
- **Typography: unknown.** Nothing recoverable.
- **Post style:** every post carries a graphic — player welcome cards,
  event flyers, memorabilia flat-lays. They've done prop-document humor
  (the Juice Kiffin "contract"). Emoji in team colors, no hashtags.

## What this means for the ads

1. Address the fanbase as **Rebel Nation**, second person. Never "fans."
2. **Hotty Toddy** is the community's password — it belongs in the
   awareness spot (04 closes with "HOTTY TODDY, REBEL NATION.").
3. The membership-drive goal number (10,000) is *their own public
   messaging* — spot 02 is on-strategy, not invented.
4. "The Sip" is available vocabulary for future cuts (e.g. a "Party in
   the 'Sip" game-day variant).
5. Tone: confident, warm, exclamatory — hype without irony. Premium
   Southern collegiate, not streetwear edge.
6. Athlete names/handles are central to THEIR feed but stay OUT of ours
   until the client clears specific NIL usage.
