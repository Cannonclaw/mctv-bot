# 60-Mile Radius Scan — What's Worth Adding

**Question:** are there markets within 60 miles of any current MCTV market worth exploring?
**Visual version:** `radius-map.html` (run `python3 handoffs/memphis-territory/build_radius_map.py`)
**Data:** `radius_scan.json` · **Status:** Iteration 5 · **Updated:** 2026-08-04

Method: great-circle distance from 31 candidate towns to all five current markets (Oxford,
Starkville, Tupelo, Columbus, West Point); nearest wins. Straight-line, not drive time.

---

## Short answer

**One real market, and it isn't in Mississippi. But the best move in range isn't a new market at
all.**

---

## 1. Tuscaloosa + Northport, AL — 53 mi from Columbus

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Population | **116,477** |
| University of Alabama | **~34,000 undergraduates** |
| Median HHI (city) | $51,464 — depressed by students, same as Oxford |
| Median HHI (metro) | $62,280 |

**The only genuine second Oxford inside the radius.** Oxford is our most proven build — 75 screens,
1 per 362 residents — and Tuscaloosa is that town at 4× the population with more students.

**Against it:**
- **Contested.** Impulse Digital Media already runs the *same free-host model* across Tuscaloosa and
  40+ Alabama cities. Trillboards has 17 programmatic screens there. Lamar owns the outdoor.
- Crosses a state line. "MCTV of MS" doesn't travel.
- Needs its own rep living in it — this is not a satellite.

**Verdict: scout it, don't commit.** Worth a trip and a competitive read. Not a build decision yet.

---

## 2. The Golden Triangle — not a new market, the best move in range

**Columbus has 2 screens. West Point has 1.** Three screens between them, while Lowndes County
absorbs:

| Project | Investment | Jobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Steel Dynamics** | **$2.5 billion** | **1,000 at an average wage of $93,000** |
| Kloeckner aluminum plant | $90M | 40 |
| Aluminum Dynamics expansion | $200M | 100 |

No territory negotiation, no new rep, existing brand, existing traction reports. Same argument as
Tupelo's under-density, with a bigger tailwind.

> **Caution.** Columbus *city* is shrinking — 22,507 people, down 6.2% since 2020, median household
> income $42,235, poverty rate 21.3%. The money is arriving in the *county* at $93,000 a job, not in
> the city's existing household base. Put screens where those wages get spent — which may mean
> Starkville and the Highway 45 corridor more than downtown Columbus. Same lesson as Byhalia: check
> on the ground before spending.

---

## 3. Philadelphia, MS — 50 mi from Starkville

Town is ~7,000, but **Pearl River Resort** next door runs three casinos, hotels, a water park and a
golf course on Choctaw tribal land, employing **2,400–3,000 people**, with MBCI operations carrying
roughly **$1B in annual economic impact**.

A destination play — a handful of high-dwell screens serving visitors, not a municipal network.
Different sale, different math. Worth a conversation with the resort, not a town build.

---

## 4. Grenada, MS — 44 mi from Oxford

12,335 people on I-55, median household income $46,303. The largest standalone town in range we
don't already touch — but a thin advertiser base.

---

## Skip

- **Corinth** (48 mi from Tupelo) — Alcorn County shrank 34,740 → 34,569 (2020→2025).
- **Holly Springs** (28 mi from Oxford) — declining -1.83%/yr, $45,462 median HHI. Close ≠ worth it.

---

## Satellite fill-in — 20 towns, ~90,000 people

None justifies its own launch. Together they're a different proposition: every one sits inside an
existing rep's drive, so the marginal cost of a few screens each is near zero — and they push the
network toward the rate-card tiers that matter.

Inside 25 miles of a market we already run: **Water Valley** (16, Oxford), **Aberdeen** (16, West
Point), **Fulton** (17, Tupelo), **Pontotoc** (17, Tupelo), **Baldwyn** (18), **Okolona** (18),
**Amory** (23), **Ackerman** (23, Starkville), **New Albany** (24), **Batesville** (25, Oxford).

Then 26–36 mi: Vernon AL, Louisville, Eupora, Bruce, Macon, Houston, Booneville, Ripley.
And 52–53 mi: Kosciusko, Winona.

## Recommendation

1. **Finish the Golden Triangle before opening anything new.** 3 screens against a $2.5B build-out.
2. **Scout Tuscaloosa.** Biggest prize in range, but already contested by our own model.
3. **Fold in satellites opportunistically** — they cost almost nothing once the rep is driving.
