What we'd build

We run 133 screens across North Mississippi today — Oxford (75), Starkville (30), Tupelo (25), Columbus (2), West Point (1). Oxford is the one to look at: 75 screens in a town of 27,000, which is the density we aim for when we own a market properly.

For the Memphis area we'd expect to start in the range of 40 screens across the first two markets, and grow from there as it proves out — realistically toward 75–90 over the first couple of years if the sell-through supports it. We'd rather build two markets properly than put a handful of screens in six.

Those are intentions, not promises — we'd want to walk the venues before committing to numbers.

Lower priority for us

Memphis proper, West Memphis and Millington aren't where we'd start, and Horn Lake we'd pick up once we're already working the county. Not a hard no on any of them — just not the first conversation.

Open questions

We've drawn this by market rather than by boundary, because we don't know where the territory lines actually fall — particularly across the state line, where several of the markets we're most interested in sit. Happy to shape the ask around whatever the real boundaries are once we understand them.
