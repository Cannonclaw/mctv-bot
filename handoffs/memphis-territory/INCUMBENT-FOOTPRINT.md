side and read from a screenshot on 2026-08-05.
**Status:** Iteration 6 — replaces the assumptions in `MARKET-MAPS.md` and `metro-map.html`
**Precision caveat:** counts are read off a screenshot at metro zoom with heavily overlapping
markers. Treat every number below as **±2**, and the clusters as more reliable than the totals.

## The finding that matters

**She is operating in Tennessee.**

Roughly **a third of her pins sit north of the state line** — a dense cluster around
**Bartlett / Lakeland**, plus three in **Germantown / East Memphis**. Those are exactly the two
zones I ranked #1 and #2 on the metro map and labeled *"TN — not DeSoto; separate n-Compass
territory."*

That label was wrong, or at least incomplete. Either her territory grant includes part of Shelby
County, or n-Compass territories are far less clean than the franchise marketing implies.

**This is now the most important question in the whole conversation** — more important than DeSoto
itself. Zone 1 (Germantown + Collierville) and Zone 2 (Bartlett + Lakeland + Arlington) hold
~179,000 people at median household incomes of **$100,660 to $149,920**. DeSoto County, the best
advertiser market in Mississippi, is $85,500.

## What this corrects

### 1. Olive Branch and Hernando are not uncontested

Every version of the build plan so far rested on one inference: *a small operator building DeSoto
would have started in Southaven and worked outward along Goodman Road, leaving Olive Branch and
Hernando open.*

**Half right.** Southaven is indeed her densest DeSoto cluster — but she has pins in Olive Branch
(~3) and Hernando (~3–4) as well.

**The build order still holds, and for a better reason than before.** Three screens in Olive Branch
is not a network; it is a toehold. Our Phase 1 target there is 24 screens. Hernando is the same
story — 3–4 pins against a 16-screen target. These are markets she has *touched*, not markets she
*owns*. The plan changes from "walk into empty ground" to "outbuild a toehold 6:1," which is a
different sales conversation but the same sequence.

### 2. She is spread thin, everywhere

~32–35 screens across **two states and seven towns**. That is not density anywhere. For scale:
Southaven alone at Oxford's proven density (1 screen per 362 residents) would carry ~160 screens;
she has ~12–14 in it.

This is consistent with what Don reportedly said about capital. It is also the single best argument
for our phased build: we are not fighting an entrenched network, we are fighting a thin one.

### 3. Marshall County is not empty either

One pin near Byhalia. Small, but it means "take Marshall County rights on paper" needs the same
exclusivity question attached as DeSoto.

## Revised questions for Don

The brief had three. The metro map added a fourth. This adds a fifth and reorders them.

1. **How is she operating in Bartlett, Lakeland and Germantown?** Does her territory include Shelby
   County, TN? If she gives up DeSoto, does the Tennessee footprint come with it — and if not, who
   holds it and is it available? **Ask this first.** It is worth more than DeSoto.
2. **Exclusive or coexistence in DeSoto?** She has live screens in all four cities we want.
3. **The NTV360 license export** — venue, address, license ID, install date, contract end date.
   Now doubly important: we need the *contract end dates* on ~32–35 licenses, because that tells us
   whether her footprint is about to free up or is locked for two more years.
4. **What build-out obligation comes attached to the grant?**
5. **Does DeSoto come under our existing agreement or a new one?** (See `COST-MODEL.md` — the
   $75/screen/month royalty is the real price, not the waived franchise fee.)

## What this does not tell us

- **Venue names.** The screenshot shows pins, not labels. The NTV360 export still matters.
- **Contract dates.** A pin is not a commitment. Some of these may be month-to-month; some may have
  a year left.
- **Whether the map is current.** It was presented as her current footprint, but we have not
  verified it against NTV360.
- **Screen counts per venue.** One pin may be one screen or several.

---

## Effect on the build plan

No change to the phase targets (40 / 75 / 90) or the tier logic. Two changes to the framing:

- **Olive Branch and Hernando go first for a revised reason** — not because they are empty, but
  because her presence there is a toehold we can outbuild quickly, while Southaven is where she is
  actually established.
- **Zone 1 and Zone 2 on the metro map are reclassified** from "separate territory, not available"
  to **"incumbent already operates here — status unknown, ask Don."** That is a much more
  interesting position than the one we thought we were in this morning.
