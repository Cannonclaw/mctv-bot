Internal — not for n-Compass
The headline, before any of the numbers below. The franchise fee is not the price of this territory. n-Compass publishes a $35,000 franchise fee — a one-time number, and the part Don is offering to waive. It also publishes an ongoing royalty of $500/month flat plus $75 per billboard per month. At 90 DeSoto screens that recurring line is roughly $87,000 every year, forever. Over five years it is about $435,000. The waived fee is on the order of 7% of what the territory actually costs to hold.
Verify this against our own agreement before using it. These are n-Compass's published franchise terms. We already run 133 screens on NTV360, so we may be on dealer terms, grandfathered terms, or a different per-screen rate entirely — and if so the published figures do not apply to us. Override the royalty inputs below with our real numbers.

How to read it

Break-even is in advertisers, not dollars. It is the number of advertisers we must have under contract, at that phase's rate, before the DeSoto operation stops losing money each month. Watch what happens to it between Phase 1 and Phase 2: the screen count nearly doubles, but break-even barely moves — because crossing 75 screens lifts the rate card from $800 to $1,300 per advertiser. Revenue per advertiser rises faster than cost per screen. That is the whole argument for phasing to the tier lines rather than stopping short of them.
Capex is the small problem. Even at full build, the hardware bill lands inside the $48,150–$120,405 range n-Compass publishes for a new franchise — which is a decent sanity check on the per-screen defaults. The recurring line is what deserves the scrutiny, and it is the one Don controls.
