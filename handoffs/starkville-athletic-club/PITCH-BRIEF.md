## 1. The meeting

Joe Underwood reached out on **Friday, August 7** asking to talk through MCTV's services in
more detail. He came to us. That is the single most important fact in this brief — this is
an inbound, not a cold call, so the job in the room is **not** to convince him that indoor
screens work. It is to make the decision easy and get a term agreed.

**Lead with the lobby board, not the screens.** The client-facing pitch
(`handoffs/starkville-athletic-club/pitch.html`) is built around it, and it is the thing that
separates us from anyone else who has ever offered to hang a TV in his gym. A gym's schedule
changes constantly and currently lives on a printed sheet that is stale by Wednesday. The
board fixes a problem he already has. The advertising network is what pays for it.

Then the free host partnership, let him ask what it costs, and hold category exclusivity back
as the close.

This is also the **first of several weeks of new-nature Starkville deals**. Whatever number
gets agreed here sets the anchor for the ones behind it. That is worth more than the
marginal dollars on this one contract.

## 5. The $199 question, answered

Creed flagged uncertainty about whether we can do the $199 chamber-style number here. The
answer is **yes — but attach it to a screen count.**

### $199 for a defined 10-screen block: good deal, holds up

| Model | Impressions/mo | CPM at $199 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conservative (`static/rates.html` v2.0) | 146,844 | **$1.36** |
| NTV360 house formula (`data/network_dashboard.json`) | 262,078 | **$0.76** |

The two models differ because the rate calculator caps repeat exposures at 6 per visit and
discounts additional screens at a venue by 75%; the traction-report formula does neither.
**Quote the conservative number.** The traction report will over-deliver against it, which is
exactly the direction you want that surprise to run.

Either way it is far under what he can buy anywhere else:

| Channel | Typical CPM |
| --- | --- |
| **MCTV at $199** | **$0.76 – $1.36** |
| Outdoor billboard | $3 – $8 |
| Radio | $5 – $12 |
| Digital display | $5 – $15 |
| Social | $6 – $12 |
| Print | $10 – $30 |
| Cable TV | $15 – $30 |

Source: `config/config.json` → `industry_benchmarks`.

### $199 for the *whole* Starkville territory: do not do it

All 31 Starkville screens at $199/mo is a **$0.40 CPM** — roughly a third of our cheapest
published tier, and an 87% discount against the $1,575/mo list the rate calculator produces
for that territory. That is not a discount, it is repricing the market. And since this is
the first of several Starkville deals, it becomes the number every other prospect hears
about.

Full territory should be **$599/mo** ($1.22 CPM), with **$499** as the floor if he prepays
12 months.

That is the close. The scarcity is real, it is verifiable, and it expires.

## 7. Objection handling

**"What's the catch? Nothing is free."**
There is no catch, and here is the business model: we make money selling the other ad slots
in the loop. Your screens make our Starkville network worth more to advertisers. You are not
the customer for the host deal — you are the reason it works.

**"I already do social media / I have a marketing person."**
Good — this is not a replacement for it. Social reaches people who already follow you. These
screens reach the ones who do not, while they are sitting still for the better part of an
hour. And your people can use every piece of content we make, anywhere they want, at no
cost. It belongs to you.

**"Let me think about it."**
Fair. Two things worth knowing before you do. The host screens cost you nothing, so there
is nothing to think over there — the only question is where they go, and we can walk the
building today. And the gym category being open is a right-now fact, not a standing offer.

**"Can you do better than $199?"**
$199 is already 43% under our published rate, and that discount exists because you are a
host partner. Where I do have room is on term — prepay six months and the seventh is free,
prepay twelve and you get the thirteenth and fourteenth. *(Per `config/config.json` →
`contract_terms`.)* Trade price for term, not price for price.

**"What if a competitor signs up later?"**
Without exclusivity, they can — we do not turn away business. That is exactly what
exclusivity buys you: a contractual commitment that we will not. And if we ever slip up,
the contract obligates us to pull the competing content within 24 hours.

**"What happens if it doesn't work?"**
Six-month minimum term, and you get a monthly traction report with real numbers — plays,
venues, impressions, cost per thousand — from our NTV360 dashboard. You will not have to
guess whether it worked.

show up as a lower headline number other prospects will hear about.

**The one genuine walk-away:** if he wants the whole Starkville territory at $199 with
exclusivity folded in, the answer is no. That is a $0.40 CPM with the category locked, and
it would set the anchor for every Starkville deal this fall. Offer the ladder instead.
