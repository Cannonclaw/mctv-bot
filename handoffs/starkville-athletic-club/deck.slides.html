Category Exclusivity
Something you should know

There is not one gym
on a Starkville screen.

Every fitness business on the MCTV network today is in Oxford or Tupelo. Not one is in Starkville.

We are opening a run of Starkville partnerships this fall, so that will not stay true forever. Category exclusivity is how you keep it that way: for as long as your agreement runs, MCTV will not put a competing gym, fitness studio, health club or personal training facility on any Starkville screen.

It goes in the contract. If we ever slip, we pull the competing ad within 24 hours and credit you for the time it ran.

Where the line falls  —  the category covers gyms and training facilities. It does not cover supplement shops, med-spas or wellness clinics. Two of those are already host venues here and will keep running their own ads. We would rather say that now than have it surprise you later.
