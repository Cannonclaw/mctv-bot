Why this matters more in a gym than anywhere else

  • Your schedule changes constantly, and right now it lives on a printed sheet that is out of date by Wednesday. The board is never out of date.
  • Members ask the front desk what time spin starts. They stop asking.
  • Holiday hours, pool closures, and a court going down for repair reach every member who walks in, the moment you post them.
  • Private bookings show the room and the time but never the name — a corporate rental or a birthday party reads simply as "Private Event."
  • On MSU game days you can put tip-off time and adjusted hours on the board and be done with it.
