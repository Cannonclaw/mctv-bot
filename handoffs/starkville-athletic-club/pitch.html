Why this matters more in your club than anywhere else

  • Your desk is staffed until six. Your members are not — they let themselves in with a key card at five in the morning and at nine at night. The board is the only thing at that desk that can still answer them.
  • A class time moves and the printed sheet is wrong until somebody prints a new one. The board is wrong for about eight seconds.
  • Holiday hours and a room closed for maintenance reach every member who walks in, the moment you post them — including the ones who come in after hours.
  • Private bookings show the room and the time but never the name — a corporate rental or a birthday party reads simply as "Private Event."
  • On MSU game days you can put adjusted hours on the board and be done with it.
