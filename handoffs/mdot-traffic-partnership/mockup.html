How the sponsorship works

No construction, no hardware, no capital outlay — the network is already built and already showing this information.

Today

MDOT data is already on air

Every screen in the network runs a road-conditions segment sourced from MDOT's public traffic feed, in every 15-minute content loop, all day.

The sponsorship

MDOT makes it official

Branded intro and outro spots bookend every play, plus a dedicated safety-message slot MDOT controls — work zones, seat belts, move-over law, weather alerts.

The reach

Drivers, before they drive

Waiting rooms, gyms, and restaurants hold people for 55 minutes on average — long enough to actually read a safety message, minutes before they get back on the road.

Built to go statewide

MCTV is North Mississippi today — and growing toward the whole state. That matters for a statewide agency.
