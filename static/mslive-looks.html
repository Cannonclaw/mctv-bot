About the tower cam look Tupelo Regional Airport already runs a live camera from the control tower at flytupelo.com/towercam, and it already credits Mississippi LIVE Weather under the picture. The airport is an MCTV host venue. Putting that picture behind the forecast makes the board something people watch, not just read.
  • It needs two yes answers: Matt's, and the airport's. Nothing from the camera goes on a screen until both say so.
  • It needs the airport's own share link. The camera is a Verkada cloud camera and the airport's page frames Verkada's player; MCTV would frame a second link issued to us, one the airport can switch off on its own, with the airport's credit under the picture.
  • Other markets get their own picture. A courthouse square, a stadium, a campus. Same layout, local view.
