Who gets what

Nobody pays anybody a fee. Each side brings what it already owns.

Mississippi LIVE gets

  • Your name on 125+ screens, all dayIn front of people streaming does not reach, at no cost to you.
  • Half of every sponsor dollarFifty percent of what is collected, paid monthly with the detail attached.
  • Control of your nameYou approve every sponsor and the look. Pull anything with a phone call.

MCTV gets

  • A weather board people trustYour name makes the sponsor slot worth more than generic data ever could.
  • Your audience pointed at our screensA launch post and on-air mentions. We hand you the graphics.

A sponsor gets

  • Presented-by bookendsA five-second intro and outro around the weather board, every play.
  • Their logo on the boardOn every screen in their market, four times an hour, twelve hours a day.
  • One seat per categoryOne roofer, one insurer, one clinic per market.
The sponsor package at launch

The Mississippi LIVE Weather segment plays once every 15-minute loop on every MCTV screen in the sponsor's market. One sponsor per category per market, and you approve each one before it airs.
