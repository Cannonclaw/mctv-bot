Screens run 12 hours a day in a 15-minute loop. A 40-screen buy at $800/month is roughly 60,000 plays — about a penny and a third per play, and less than the cost of forty tickets to a single show.

What we're asking for

Three things, none of which require new creative from the arena:

  • Add MCTV to the announcement list, so we see a show the day it's announced.
  • Send us the tour art you already get from the promoter — we build the spot.
  • Pick a market. Tupelo alone, or all five for the shows worth driving to.
